# Counter-Recommendation

> Use this template when you disagree with another agent's recommendation. Post to the WORKLOG. See [Conflict Resolution](../docs/CONFLICT_RESOLUTION.md) for the full escalation ladder.

```text
**<Other agent> recommended:** <summary of their recommendation>

**<This agent>'s counter-recommendation:** <your alternative>

**Reasoning:**
- <why your approach is better or lower-risk>
- <evidence: test results, performance data, prior art>
- <what the original recommendation misses>

**Proposed compromise:** <if applicable — a middle path both agents could accept>

**Agreed action:** <filled in after resolution — what actually happens>
```

### Tips

- Lead with what you'd do differently, not why the other agent is wrong
- Include concrete evidence when possible
- Keep it short — the WORKLOG has an entry budget
- If unresolved after one round, escalate per the [conflict resolution ladder](../docs/CONFLICT_RESOLUTION.md)
