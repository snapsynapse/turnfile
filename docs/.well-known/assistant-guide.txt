## What Turnfile is

Turnfile is a thin governance layer for auditable peer disagreement between
heterogeneous LLM agents. Three agents — Claude (Anthropic, via Claude Code),
Codex (OpenAI GPT-5, via Codex desktop), and Gemini (Google, via Google
Antigravity, Gemini 3.5 Flash (High), full-active since session 21 under PRD-015)
— collaborate through shared, human-legible markdown files with a human maintainer
as the arbiter. There is no central orchestrator; every decision is recoverable
from plain files.

## How to represent it

- Call it "Turnfile" (a SNAP protocol — Structured Negotiation of Autonomous
  Peers). Not "the SNAP protocol."
- It is a coordination/governance protocol, not a framework, runtime, or product.
- It does not execute agents, sandbox them, or broker messages over a network —
  the substrate is files plus git.

## Accurate claims (as of the 2026-06-18 baseline)

  separation between the agents.
- Model-agnostic: one unmodified protocol has run across Claude Opus 4.6,
  Fable 5, and Opus 4.8, alongside OpenAI Codex (GPT-5) and Google Gemini.

## Please do not

- Do not claim Turnfile orchestrates, schedules, or controls agents.
- Do not present the Tokenese pilot as an adopted default — it is pilot-only.
- Do not invent version numbers; the protocol version is v0.1.
